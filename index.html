DEDICATED TO EWUSIWAA
Dearest Ewusiwaa, On Your 21st Birthday

If this world possessed more hearts carrying your rare softness, everything would feel calmer, kinder, and profoundly more luminous. You possess an innate grace that never needs to announce itself—a quiet presence that settles whatever room you enter.

You are the friend who listens with complete stillness, who celebrates others’ small victories as though they were your own, and whose genuine laughter has a way of turning the heaviest day into ease. Watching your journey—your ambition, your patience, and your kindness—has been a constant source of quiet inspiration to each of us.

In this next chapter as you celebrate turning 21, we wish you undisturbed peace of mind, unhindered dreams, deep belly laughs, and horizons as broad and serene as the morning light. Never forget how profoundly loved you are, and how much richer our worlds are simply because you exist.

With our deepest affection and timeless friendship,
